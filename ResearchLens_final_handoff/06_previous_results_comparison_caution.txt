Comparison with previous paper — status and caution
The available prior summary files report BM25 Precision@10 = 0.20 and semantic Precision@10 = 0.26, Recall@10 = 0.251 / 0.549, and F1@10 = 0.212 / 0.304. These figures are from an older metrics summary and were not recomputed from the current exact five-query run. An earlier run also used an incorrect fifth query (“Deep learning for climate prediction”), so that run is not directly comparable.
Current provisional label file yields macro P@10 of 0.48 for BM25 and 0.42 for semantic retrieval; defined-query-only binary nDCG@10 averages are 0.961152 and 0.867839 respectively. These are not final validated results because all 100 audit statuses remain “Needs human confirmation” and Audited_Relevant is blank. Do not claim improvement over the paper until labels and metric conventions are audited consistently.
