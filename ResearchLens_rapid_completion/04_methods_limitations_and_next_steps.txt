ResearchLens — 2-hour completion package
IMPORTANT: This package does NOT claim a completed independent human relevance audit. Labels in 01 are AI-assisted suggestions from title + abstract; uncertain items remain blank. The column Audited_Relevant contains only the high-confidence AI suggestions, and every populated row explicitly says human verification is still required. Do not represent these as human-audited ground truth.

Files:
01_audit_labels_AI_assisted.csv — 100 retrieved results, suggestion labels and unresolved items flagged.
02_metrics_per_query_PROVISIONAL.csv — per-query Precision@10/nDCG@10 computed provisionally; unresolved labels treated as 0, so this is a conservative placeholder, not final.
03_metrics_macro_PROVISIONAL.csv — method-level macro summary.
04_methods_limitations_and_next_steps.txt — definitions, known limitations and how to finalize.

Scope:
5 approved queries; two retrieval methods (BM25 and Semantic MiniLM + FAISS); top 10 each; 100 total results. Retrieval is domain-restricted. Dataset 2,200 records (220 per domain), embedding shape 2,200 x 384.

Precision@10 = relevant results among first 10 / 10.
Binary nDCG@10 = DCG@10 / ideal DCG@10; undefined when there are no relevant results in top 10.
Recall@10/F1 are not reported as final because corpus-wide relevant totals (all 220 papers in each query's domain) have not been audited.
