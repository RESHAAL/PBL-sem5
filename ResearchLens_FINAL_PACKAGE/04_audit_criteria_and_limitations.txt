ResearchLens relevance audit — 30 October 2026

This is an AI-assisted title-and-abstract review of 100 retrieved rows (5 queries × 2 methods × top 10). It is not independent human verification.

Strict query-specific criteria:
1. AI for early disease detection: a specific disease/clinical event and detection, prediction, screening, or diagnosis task must be explicit; generic healthcare AI does not qualify.
2. Machine learning for crop disease prediction: plant/crop/leaf disease detection, classification, prediction, or directly related pest detection/management must be explicit.
3. Explainable AI for cybersecurity: both explainability/interpretability and a cybersecurity task must be explicit.
4. AI-based personalized learning: personalized or adaptive learning must be a clear focus.
5. AI for climate prediction: climate/weather forecasting or prediction must be explicit; pollution, emissions, water quality, or sustainability alone do not qualify.

Metrics: Precision@10 = relevant results in top 10 / 10. nDCG@10 uses binary relevance and logarithmic rank discounts. nDCG is undefined when no relevant item is retrieved in the top 10 (ideal DCG = 0). Macro nDCG excludes undefined queries and reports the number of included queries.

Recall and corpus-wide F1 are not reported because all candidate documents have not been relevance-judged. Human confirmation of all 100 labels is still recommended before calling the results validated. Zero relevant results for a query should be treated as a retrieval/corpus coverage issue to investigate, not evidence that the method can never retrieve relevant material.
